Lumi privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who uses Lumi

Lumi is a private assistant run by one household for its own members. It is not offered to the public. Only Google accounts that belong to the household are connected to it.

What Lumi can access

With the permissions each person grants, Lumi can read and change that person's Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, Google Drive files (including Docs and Sheets) and Google Contacts. It can also send email from Lumi's own Gmail account. It does not read anyone's inbox.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out what a family member asks for, such as adding an event, listing to-dos or writing a note. It is processed on a server at home. To produce an answer, the request and the related data (for example, the events being asked about) are sent to the AI model provider that Lumi uses. They are not used for anything else.

Lumi does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not share it with anyone else. Data obtained from Google APIs is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

Access tokens are stored only on the home server. Anyone can remove Lumi's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that, Lumi can no longer reach the account.
